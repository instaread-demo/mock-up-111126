25 players called into November 2026 camp

US Soccer announced the 25-player roster for the November 2026 USMNT camp on Wednesday, November 4. The November window begins on November 9 and runs through the 17. The United States plays Haiti in the Nations League quarterfinals, with Haiti’s home game at Miami’s Nu Stadium on November 14 (4:30pm ET – TBS) and the USMNT hosting at Cincinnati’s TQL Stadium on November 17 (7pm ET – TBS).

“We are excited to bring this group together at the beginning of our journey in this important competition,” USMNT coach Mauricio Pochettino said in the press statement announcing the roster. “These players have earned the opportunity to compete and help achieve our main objective, which is to win and advance in the tournament.”

UPDATE: 11/6: Mark McKenzie replaces George Campbell due to injury. 11/7: Jack McGlynn replaces Gio Reyna due to injury.

November 2026 roster

GK: Matt Freese (New York City FC; 20/0), Diego Kochen (Lyngby Boldklub/DEN; 2/0), Brian Schwake (Nashville SC; 2/0)
DEF: Reed Baker-Whiting (Nashville SC; 0/0), George Campbell (West Bromwich Albion FC/ENG; 3/0), Sergiño Dest (PSV Eindhoven/NED; 46/3), Alex Freeman (Villarreal/ESP; 26/3), Neil Pierre (Philadelphia Union; 3/0), Bryan Reynolds (Stade Rennais/FRA; 7/1), Chris Richards (Crystal Palace/ENG; 43/4), Antonee Robinson (Fulham/ENG; 62/5), Auston Trusty (Celtic/SCO; 14/1)
MID: Tyler Adams (AFC Bournemouth/ENG; 62/2), Mathis Albert (Borussia Dortmund/GER; 4/1), Sebastian Berhalter (Middlesbrough/ENG; 22/4), Santiago Castañeda (SC Paderborn/GER; 0/0), Weston McKennie (Juventus/ITA; 71/12), Adri Mehmeti (Red Bull New York; 3/0), Gio Reyna (RC Strasbourg Alsace/FRA; 47/10), Malik Tillman (Bayer Leverkusen/GER; 39/7)
FOR: Folarin Balogun (Monaco/FRA; 31/12), Justin Ellis (Orlando City SC; 3/2), Julian Hall (Red Bull New York; 4/3), Christian Pulisic (AC Milan/ITA; 90/33), Cavan Sullivan (Philadelphia Union; 3/1)
