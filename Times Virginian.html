Veterans Day 2026, honoring American Veterans & Current military personnel, will be observed on Wednesday, November 11. On this day, retail stores, major corporations & large restaurant chains will offer free meals, coffee, and various other special discounts to veterans & active-duty service members.

Every year prominent companies & brands announce exclusive offers including brands such as Applebee’s, Starbucks, Denny’s, and Chipotle. Many other major retail stores & restaurants also provide special discounts to veterans and military personnel. To know more about the Veterans Day 2026 discounts, free meals and other offers read complete information below.

Quick Answer: Veterans Day 2026 will be observed on Wednesday, November 11. Eligible veterans and active-duty service members can get free meals, coffee and shopping discounts at Applebee’s, Starbucks, Target and more. Check the details below for the full offers list and required documents.

2026 Veterans Day Free Meals, Discounts Offers at Restaurants and Stores

This is a special day for eligible Veterans & Active-duty military workers, as many restaurants offer them free meals. This year, Applebee’s is providing eligible veterans with a free, full-sized entree from their menu. Additionally, they will get a $5 Bounce Back Card to use during a future visit.

Meanwhile, at Denny’s, eligible veterans and active-duty military members can enjoy a free ‘Original Grand Slam’ between 5 AM to 12 PM. Similarly, Red Lobster is offering these service members & veterans a free ‘Shrimp & Chips’ meal.

On this special Veterans Day, Golden Corral is hosting a ‘Military Appreciation Night,’ where eligible individuals can enjoy a free buffet. Furthermore, at select Chili’s locations, exclusively for this special day, eligible individuals can choose a free entree from the menu.

However, all Veterans should keep in mind that the specific terms & availability of these offers may vary from one restaurant to another.

Veterans Day 2026 Free Coffee & Shopping Deals

To avail all special offers & discounts available on Wednesday, 2026, one must meet the eligibility criteria. On this day, several major American companies are offering free coffee, drinks & shopping discounts.

Through these offers, military personnel can purchase items of their choice at a lower cost or even for free. Some of the offers available on this special day are as follows:

Free Coffee & Drink Offers

  • At Starbucks, eligible personnel can receive a free 12-ounce hot or iced brewed coffee.
  • Meanwhile, at Circle K, a medium hot or iced coffee will be available for free.
  • Additionally, military personnel can enjoy a free hot coffee at Wawa, and they can also get a free 16-ounce original lemonade at Hot Dog on a Stick.

Here are some special shopping discounts available to veterans:

  • Veterans can receive a 20% discount on eligible purchases at ‘Target’, as well as a 30% discount at Kohl’s on November 10th and 11th.
  • Furthermore, veterans can avail of a 10% discount on groceries at Publix, and a 10% discount on purchases at Food Lion.
  • Eligible veterans can also get a 20% discount when purchasing footwear at Rack Room Shoes.

Who Can Get Veterans Day 2026 Discounts & Offers

Not all U.S. citizens are eligible for these special offers; they are available exclusively to Veterans & active-duty service members. Additionally, some companies are extending these offers to include members of the National Guard, military spouses & reservists.

To avail of these offers, veterans may be asked to provide the following documentation –

  • A valid military ID card or a veteran ID card.
  • Alternatively, a health identification card or DD214 discharge papers may be requested.
  • A state-issued driver’s license with a veteran designation will also be accepted to qualify for these benefits.

More Updates On Veterans Day 2026 Discounts

For this special day, many companies are offering special deals, such as discounts on haircuts or coupon cards for outings, specifically for veterans. Veterans and currently serving military personnel can get their cars washed for free at car wash centers participating in the ‘Grace for Vets’ initiative. For more information regarding these offers, you can visit the official ‘Grace for Vets’ website.

FAQs

When will ‘Veterans Day 2026’ take place?

In the US, Veterans Day 2026 will be observed on Wednesday, November 11.

Which restaurants are offering free meals to veterans on Veterans Day 2026?

Several restaurants, including Applebee’s, Denny’s, Red Lobster, Golden Corral, and Shake Shack, are offering free meals.

Do I need a military ID to take advantage of these offers?

Yes, most establishments require a military ID or other valid proof of military service, so you will need to have it with you.
