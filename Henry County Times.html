Roark passes away at age 58

Seth Jackson    November 22, 2025 November 22, 2025    Comments Off on Roark passes away at age 58
Listen to audio version of this article
Sponsored by

We were very saddened to hear of the  death of Monroe Roark. He was a contributing writer for the Henry County Times for many years.

ReadNewspapers

The Times appreciated Monroe for his dependability and dedication to proficient, fair  journalism. His concise descriptions gave readers a front row seat to Henry County news and events. He was an honest and direct reporter.

We will always remember his friendship and his willingness to bring quality reporting to Henry County. 

He will be missed.
