As America’s ‘First to Fight’ defense force, the U.S. Marine Corps continues to set the standard for readiness, resolve, and warfighting excellence. 

For 251 years, Marines have stood ready to fight and win our Nation’s battles. Since before the first days of the American Revolution to the engagements of today, the Marine Corps has served as a decisive naval and amphibious force, projecting power, defending our interests, and helping turn the tide in conflicts around the world. Across every clime and place, Marines remain ready to be the first to fight whenever and wherever our Nation needs them.  
Happy Birthday, Marines, from a very proud Marine Corps Combat Veteran wife.
