Scranton withstands Kingston frantic comeback

Published 12:00 am Tuesday, November 10, 2026

By Staff Report sports@harrisondaily.com

KINGSTON — The Kingston Lady Yellow Jackets nearly doubled their score in the fourth period.

Playing Scranton, the squad came up just short and lost to the Lady Rockets, 52-47.
Scranton took control of the game in the first half.
The Lady Rockets led 17-10 at the first break and 31-16 at halftime.
The Lady Yellow Jackets held Scranton to six points in the third period and then cut two points off of the lead.
In the decisive fourth period, Kingston came up short in picking up the victory.
Leading Kingston in scoring was Kylee Weaver with 20 points. Leelou Kilbourn added 11, Keirra Williams eight, Mesa Villines four, Clara Huber three and Whitlee Stacey one.
